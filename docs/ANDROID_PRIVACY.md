# RecipeCove for Android — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

RecipeCove for Android is a companion to the self-hosted RecipeCove recipe manager. It connects to the RecipeCove server that you choose. It is not a hosted recipe-account service operated centrally by the Android app developer.

## Information used by the Android app

To work with your selected server, the Android app processes its address, your account session, and recipe data available to your account. This can include recipe titles, ingredients, instructions, images, notes, household meal plans, and shopping-list items.

If you choose to share a link, image, video, or text from another Android app, RecipeCove processes that selection and can upload it to your selected server for recipe import. Shared material is not sent unless you choose to share it.

## Storage and security

RecipeCove keeps its server session token in app-private storage protected with Android Keystore-based encryption. Android backup is disabled for this app. A person administering your self-hosted server may access and retain information stored on that server according to the server's configuration.

Use HTTPS when connecting over the public internet. Local-network HTTP is supported for privately operated servers.

## Data sharing, analytics and advertising

The Android application does not contain an advertising SDK or a third-party analytics SDK. It exchanges account and recipe information with your configured RecipeCove server as required for its features. A server you choose may in turn access third-party recipe or social-media sites when you explicitly request an import. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

RecipeCove does not sell user data.

## Deleting information

You can sign out of the app and clear locally saved app data using Android Settings. Recipe or account data held on the self-hosted server must be deleted through that server's account/administration controls, or by contacting its operator. Removing the Android app does not delete server-side records or backups.
